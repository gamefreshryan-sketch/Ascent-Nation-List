What is ANL?

ANL (Ascent Nation List) is a list of the hardest levels made and verified by members of the Ascent Nation, created by Ascent, M1xxronium, and fellow community members.

Points System

  • Main List (#1 - #50): Levels reward points on completion. #1 awards 350.00 pts.
  • Extended List (#51+): Free to beat and records are recorded, but award 0.00 pts.
  • Verifier Bonus: Verifiers receive base placement points PLUS a 20% bonus!

Rules for Level Submissions

  1. Must be at least 25 seconds long.
  2. No automated levels (No Auto Retray).
  3. Offensive, racist, or sexist levels result in an instant ban.
  4. Stolen or copied levels are forbidden.
  5. Proof of legitimacy is required (clicks/taps, raw video proof).
  6. NONG songs are allowed if they don't contain heavily sensitive lyrics.
