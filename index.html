What is ANL?

ANL (Ascent Nation List) is a list of the hardest levels made and verified by members of the Ascent Nation (Ascent_GD's community), created by Ascent, M1xxronium, and fellow community members.

Points System

  • Main List (#1 - #50): Levels reward points on completion. #1 awards 350.00 pts, tapering down to position #50.
  • Extended List (#51+): Free to beat and records are tracked, but award 0.00 pts.
  • Verification Bonus: Verifiers receive the full base placement points PLUS a 20% bonus! (e.g., verifying a 350 pt #1 level gives 350 + 70 = 420.00 pts).

How to get a level placed?

  1. Length: Must be at least 25 seconds long (few exceptions apply).
  2. No Auto Levels: No automated levels (Sorry, no Auto Retray).
  3. Strict Conduct: Offensive, racist, or sexist levels are strictly rejected and can lead to a community ban.
  4. Originality: Stolen levels or copies of existing rated levels will not be accepted.
  5. Proof of Legitimacy: Raw footage, clicks/taps, and legitimate completion proof are required to avoid unverified impossible difficulty.
  6. NONGs: Allowed, provided songs don't contain heavily sensitive content or lyrics.
