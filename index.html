What is ANL?

ANL (Ascent Nation List) is a list of the hardest levels created and verified by members of the Ascent Nation community.

Points & Percentages System

  • Main List (#1 - #50): Levels reward points. Rank #1 awards a maximum base of 350.00 pts.
  • Extended List (#51+): Records are documented and accepted, but award 0.00 pts.
  • List Requirement %: Levels can specify a minimum qualifying percentage (e.g., 60%). Qualifying progress records award between 10% and 50% of the level's point value.
  • Full Completions (100%): Award 100% of the level's points.
  • Verifier Bonus: Verifiers receive base placement points PLUS a 20% bonus!

Rules for Submissions

  1. Must be at least 25 seconds long.
  2. No automated levels or physics bypasses.
  3. Audible clicks/taps and full unedited footage are required for top-tier records.
  4. Each user has a maximum limit of 3 simultaneous pending submissions to prevent queue flooding.
  5. Submitting fraudulent, spliced, or hacked footage will lead to permanent list blacklisting.
