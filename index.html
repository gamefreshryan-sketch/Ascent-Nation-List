What is ANL?

ANL (Ascent Nation List) is a list of the hardest levels created and verified by members of the Ascent Nation community.

Points & Percentages System

  • Main List (#1 - #50): Levels reward points. Rank #1 awards a maximum base of 350.00 pts.
  • Extended List (#51+): Records are documented and accepted, but award 0.00 pts.
  • List Requirement %: Levels can specify a minimum qualifying percentage (e.g., 60%). Qualifying progress records award between 10% and 50% of the level's point value depending on progress.
  • Full Completions (100%): Award 100% of the level's points.
  • Verifier Bonus: Verifiers receive base placement points PLUS a 20% bonus!

Rules for Level Submissions

  1. Must be at least 25 seconds long.
  2. No automated levels (No Auto Retray).
  3. Offensive or abusive levels result in an instant ban.
  4. Stolen or copied levels are strictly forbidden.
  5. Proof of legitimacy is required (clicks/taps, full raw unedited footage).
  6. NONG songs are allowed if they don't contain heavily sensitive audio.
