⭐ Level Specifications

What is ANL?

ANL (Ascent Nation List) is the official list of the hardest levels created and verified by members of the Ascent Nation community.

Points & Open Verification System

  • Main List (#1 - #50): Levels reward points. Rank #1 awards a maximum base of 350.00 pts.
  • Extended List (#51+): Records are documented and accepted, but award 0.00 pts.
  • Open Verification Space: Unverified levels open to community verification. Contains 0.00 pts and no minimum requirement until officially verified and placed on the main/extended list!
  • Progress on Open Levels: Anyone can submit a progress record as long as it surpasses the current World Record (WR).
  • Verifier Bonus: Verifiers receive placement points PLUS a 20% bonus!

Rules for Submissions

  1. The level must be visually interesting. Some sense of investment to building a level is expected.
  2. Every level must include a valid Geometry Dash Level ID.
  3. CBF is allowed.
  4. No physics bypasses or automated inputs.
  5. Audible clicks/taps and raw unedited footage are required for top-tier records.
  6. Limit of 3 simultaneous pending submissions per account.

Origin of Ascent Nation List

As I began streaming Geometry Dash consistently, our passionate community grew rapidly. To bring everyone together under one roof, I founded our official Discord community: The Ascent Nation.

Before long, talented creators, verifiers, and challenge runners in our Discord started creating custom levels and hard-hitting challenges specifically for our community.

Our List Philosophy:

Levels on ANL do not need to be rated by RobTop, nor do they need to meet standard length criteria. The only universal rule: It must be verified 100% LEGIT.
