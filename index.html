⭐ New Level Information

What is ANL?

ANL (Ascent Nation List) is a list of the hardest levels created and verified by members of the Ascent Nation community.

Points & Percentages System

  • Main List (#1 - #50): Levels reward points. Rank #1 awards a maximum base of 350.00 pts.
  • Extended List (#51+): Records are documented and accepted, but award 0.00 pts.
  • List Requirement %: Levels specify a minimum qualifying percentage. Qualifying progress records award between 10% and 50% of the level's point value.
  • Full Completions (100%): Award 100% of the level's points.
  • Verifier Bonus: Verifiers receive placement points PLUS a 20% bonus!

Rules for Submissions

  1. The level must be visually interesting. Some sense of investment to building a level is expected.
  2. TOP 51+ level submissions will NOT be prioritized. (Still accepted though.)
  3. CBF is allowed.
  4. No physics bypasses or automated inputs.
  5. Audible clicks/taps and raw unedited footage are required for top-tier records.
  6. Limit of 3 simultaneous pending submissions per account.

Lost your account?

  1. Ascent Nation List does not store personal emails. Keep your password safe. If you lose your credentials, open an account recovery ticket in our Discord.

Origin of Ascent Nation List

As I began streaming Geometry Dash consistently, our passionate community grew rapidly. To bring everyone together under one roof, I founded our official Discord community: The Ascent Nation.

Before long, talented creators, verifiers, and challenge runners in our Discord started creating custom levels and hard-hitting challenges specifically for our community.

Our List Philosophy:

Levels on ANL do not need to be rated by RobTop, nor do they need to meet standard length criteria (they can be challenges, short levels, or full-length epics). The only universal rule: It must be verified 100% LEGIT.

This website was designed and launched to properly document, rank, and celebrate every legitimate victor and level born from our community.
