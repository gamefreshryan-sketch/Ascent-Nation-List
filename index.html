What is ANL?

ANL (Ascent Nation List) is a list of the hardest levels created and verified by members of the Ascent Nation community.

Submission Guidelines & Rules

  • Queue Limit: Players can have a maximum of 3 submissions on hold at any given time to avoid spam.
  • Queue Position: Submissions are processed on a First-In, First-Out (FIFO) basis. Your queue position indicates how many submissions are in front of yours.
  • Verification & Completions: Both verifications and progress runs (≥ list req) must be accompanied by raw unedited footage and audible clicks/taps.
  • Denial Policy: Submissions rejected for hacking, missing clicks, or abusive material can result in submit restrictions (1 day to 1 month) or full list bans.
